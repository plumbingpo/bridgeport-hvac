Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Danbury

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
